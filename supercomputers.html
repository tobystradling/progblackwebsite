Applications of Supercomputers

Weather Forecasting

Supercomputers analyse weather data to predict storms, floods, and other extreme weather events. They also help scientists study climate change and predict future climate patterns.

Medicine and Scientific Research

Researchers use supercomputers to develop new medicines, study diseases, and analyse genetic information. They are also used to simulate space and scientific phenomena.

Engineering

Engineers use supercomputers to test designs for cars, aircraft, and buildings through simulations. This improves safety, efficiency, and reduces development costs.

Artificial Intelligence

Supercomputers provide the processing power needed to train AI models. They analyse large datasets, recognise patterns, and help develop advanced technologies.
